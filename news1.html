New Technologies Are Expanding the Future of Space Exploration

Space technology is developing rapidly as scientists and engineers work on new ways to explore the Moon and beyond.

NASA is currently seeking new technologies for lunar exploration, including systems for power generation, oxygen extraction and producing materials on the Moon. These technologies could help support future long-term lunar missions.

Space technology is also being developed for spacecraft, communication systems, scientific instruments and robotic exploration.
