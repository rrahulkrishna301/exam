NVIDIA Continues to Expand AI Computing Technology

NVIDIA is one of the major companies developing computing hardware and software for artificial intelligence applications.

The company is developing new AI computing platforms designed to provide the processing power required by modern artificial intelligence systems.

NVIDIA has also recently announced developments involving its Vera Rubin platform and AI infrastructure, as well as new work on quantum computing through its CUDA-Q platform.
