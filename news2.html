OpenAI Introduces a New Generation of AI Technology

OpenAI is continuing to develop artificial intelligence systems designed to help people with research, programming, writing, analysis and other tasks.

OpenAI recently announced GPT-6 Astra, describing it as a new generation of intelligence. The company has also introduced developments including new voice experiences and the Agents API.

These developments show how artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly useful for both individuals and organisations.
